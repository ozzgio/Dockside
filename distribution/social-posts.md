## LinkedIn

I built Dockside to make the small screen on my Cabletime ScreenDock more useful on a Mac.

It’s a native dashboard with a clear clock, live Mac activity, a focus timer, seven themes, and shortcuts for the apps you actually use. A separate Control Center handles fullscreen, appearance, and setup.

This is an early developer preview. It still needs the vendor’s USB Display driver, and the download isn’t notarized yet. The source is public; I haven’t chosen the license yet.

I’m also documenting an initial interoperability investigation of the vendor driver. No replacement driver is available yet.

Project: https://ozzgio.github.io/Dockside/
Source, downloads, and feedback: https://github.com/ozzgio/Dockside

If you use one of these little dock displays on a Mac, I’d be interested in your feedback.
