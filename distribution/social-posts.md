## LinkedIn

I built Dockside to make the small screen on my Cabletime ScreenDock more useful on a Mac.

It’s a native dashboard with a clear clock, live Mac activity, optional focus, seven themes, and shortcuts for the apps you actually use. A separate Control Center handles fullscreen, appearance, and setup.

This is an early developer preview. It now has experimental native USB for my tested 1280 × 720 dock, with the factory app stopped. The download isn’t notarized yet. The source is public; I haven’t chosen the license yet.

The native mode is dashboard-only, uses a private macOS display API, and needs broader hardware and sleep/wake testing. The original driver remains available as a fallback.

Project: https://ozzgio.github.io/Dockside/
Source, downloads, and feedback: https://github.com/ozzgio/Dockside

If you use one of these little dock displays on a Mac, I’d be interested in your feedback.
