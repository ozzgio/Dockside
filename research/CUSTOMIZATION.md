# Small-display customization references

The product direction favors large, readable content and selectable widgets on a fixed 1280 × 720 panel.

- [Übersicht's widget model](https://tracesof.net/uebersicht/) informed independent widget visibility rather than one mandatory dashboard composition. Dockside implements its own native widgets; it does not execute downloaded widget scripts.
- [Divoom's display/dashboard descriptions](https://divoom.com/pages/faqs) and [Pixoo dashboard example](https://divoom.com/blogs/app-guide/pixoo-64-home-assistant-smart-home-dashboard) show the value of glanceable clock faces and choosing which information belongs on a desk screen. Dockside uses three starting layouts, seven saved palettes, and a larger adjustable clock.

These are product references, not software dependencies or endorsements. Their artwork, code, and proprietary integrations are not included. Weather/accounts, financial feeds, arbitrary third-party widgets, and a cloud theme marketplace are outside this implementation.
